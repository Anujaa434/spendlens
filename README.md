
# SpendLens 🔍

> Find out exactly where your AI tool budget is leaking — and how to fix it.

SpendLens is a free AI spend auditor for developers and engineering managers. 
Input the AI tools you pay for, and get an instant breakdown of where you're 
overspending, what to switch or downgrade, and your total potential monthly savings.

Built as a lead-generation tool for [Credex](https://credex.rocks) — the platform 
for discounted AI infrastructure credits.

---

## Who it's for

- **Solo developers** paying for Cursor, Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT out of pocket
- **Engineering managers** at small startups whose team uses 3-5 AI tools with no visibility into whether they're on the right plans
